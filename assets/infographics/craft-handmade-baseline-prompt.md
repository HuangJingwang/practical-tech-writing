Create one polished Chinese technical editorial infographic as a warm handmade paper-craft illustration.

Specifications:
- Use case: infographic-diagram. A blog article's opening visual overview.
- Style: baoyu-infographic / craft-handmade, blending hand-drawn cartoon diagrams and very subtle layered paper cutouts.
- Layout: linear-progression, wrapped into two rows of three stages, 4:3 landscape canvas, high resolution preferably 2048 x 1536 or larger at the same aspect ratio.
- Audience: adult technical readers. Friendly and restrained, not a children's poster.
- Main point: each new approach addresses a concrete limitation left by earlier approaches. Preserve the technical mechanisms and sparse content.

Style guidance:
Warm cream paper background #FFF8F0, almost imperceptible paper grain, ample whitespace. Slightly imperfect dark charcoal pencil/ink outlines; soft pastel paper fills in muted sky blue, sage green, apricot and restrained coral accents. Delicate paper-cut edges and shallow soft shadows, a little translucent paper tape is allowed at the main title only. All imagery must look hand-drawn and illustrated, never photorealistic. Use large, extremely legible Chinese handwriting-style labels, consistent line weight and crisp dark text. No heavy boxed dashboard, no thick panels, no giant cartoon character, no elaborate decoration, no cluttered textures, no watermark. Visual diagrams do most of the explaining.

Composition:
Large title at top left: "从 RAG 到 OKF + Git"
Small subtitle just below: "上一步的问题，推动下一步改进"
Below the header place six spacious illustrated stations, each with a small numbered circle, a bold technical name above, an actual miniature diagram in the middle, and ONE short line below explaining its contribution.
Reading order: top row 1 -> 2 -> 3, then a clean hand-drawn arrow travels through the wide middle gutter from stage 3 on the upper right to stage 4 on the lower left, then bottom row 4 -> 5 -> 6. Keep the wrapping connector distinct from every internal diagram. The cause labels belong ON THE GAPS between stations, not inside the wrong station. Generous margins, no crossed labels.

Caption: "接入模型之外的资料"

Cause from 1 to 2: "关系难串联"
